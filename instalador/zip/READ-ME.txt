MEIBOX - how to install
=======================

A home screen for the R36S, with Mei the cat, your games and your play stats.
Works on the R36S with dArkOS or ArkOS. No internet or cable needed: just the SD card.

INSTALL
1. Switch the device off and take out the system SD card.
2. Put the card in your computer and open the drive called EASYROMS (where the games are).
3. Copy the "tools" and "totobox" folders from this ZIP into EASYROMS.
   If the computer asks to merge or replace, say yes.
4. Put the card back and switch the device on.
5. In EmulationStation, open Options > Tools > Instalar Meibox and follow the screen.
   Pick "English" on the first screen. (A confirms, B goes back, D-pad moves.)
6. At the end it offers to restart. From then on, Meibox opens at boot.

To skip Meibox once: hold B while the device starts.
To go to EmulationStation: press B on the Meibox welcome screen.

FASTER GAMES (recommended)
The installer asks whether to make games run faster. It swaps three emulators
(snes9x, mGBA and gpSP) for versions rebuilt for the R36S and tunes video game by
game. Measured on an R36S: SNES 24% faster on average, GBA 11% to 280%, and the
button responds one frame sooner. Games are recognised by their content, not by
file name. Remove puts everything back.
Licenses and source code for these emulators: totobox/instalador/desempenho/licencas.

MEI COPILOT (optional)
In-game hints from an AI: press SELECT + R1 and Mei tells you what to do.
Needs a USB Wi-Fi adapter (the R36S has no Wi-Fi) and an OpenAI or Anthropic API key
(each hint costs a fraction of a cent).
Before step 4, open EASYROMS/totobox/chave-da-ia.txt on the computer, paste your key
on the empty line and save. The installer moves the key somewhere safe on the device
and erases it from the card.
Installed without a key? Put it in the file and run Instalar Meibox again.

UPDATE OR REMOVE
Copy a newer version's folders over the old ones and run Options > Tools > Instalar Meibox.
The same item has a Remove option, which also restores the original emulators.
Your pictures, lines, hints and guides stay in the totobox folder on the card.

WANT AN AI TO DO IT?
If you use an AI assistant that works on your computer (Claude Code, Codex and the like),
connect the device to Wi-Fi and ask it to read PARA-SUA-IA.md from this ZIP. It installs
over the network and explains each step.

PROBLEMS?
The last install log is EASYROMS/totobox/instalador/ultimo-log.txt (it never contains
your key). Open an issue at https://github.com/carbiecarbie/meibox with that file.
